# My agent: recipe-assistant
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps home cooks discover and customize recipes with a catalog of dishes, dietary memory, and food visuals.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: User dietary restrictions, favorite cuisines, pantry ingredients, and skill level.
- Tools: Recipe search, nutrition lookup, and unit conversions.
- Catalog/UI: Collection of recipes rendered as rich cards and ingredients lists as tables.
- Image gen: Custom food and presentation images for generated recipes.
- Sandbox: Macro-nutrient splits and scaling recipe yield calculations.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): code sandbox for scaling recipe yields and computing macro-nutrients
